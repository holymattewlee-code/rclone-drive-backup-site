Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

This is a personal-use OAuth application for Rclone, used by its owner.

The app requests read-only access to Google Drive. Rclone downloads selected files to the owner's local computer.

Downloaded files and OAuth credentials are not stored on this website. OAuth tokens are managed locally by Rclone.

This website does not operate analytics or user registration. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process visitor data under GitHub's privacy policy.

The owner may revoke Google Drive access through Google Account settings.

For inquiries, use the developer contact shown on the OAuth consent screen.
